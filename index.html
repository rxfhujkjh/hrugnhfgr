About this scorecard

How the comparison math works
  • CD comparison: each deposit is counted at the CD rate from the day it went in, up to today. Money taken out is not subtracted from the comparison.
  • What it's worth in total: today's account value (holdings at current prices + cash) plus each amount taken out, grown at the savings rate from the day it left. Money taken out is counted exactly once — here, on the actual side.
  • Amount needed: CD comparison minus what it's worth in total. If that number is positive, that is the amount needed now to match the CD. If it is negative, the account is ahead of the CD and nothing is needed — anything above stays in the account.
  • Dividends need no special handling: reinvested dividends show up as purchases (they're in the holdings), and dividends paid into savings show up as money taken out (counted above, at the savings rate).
  • Yearly return so far: — This is the yearly pace of the account so far, for comparison with the CD rate. It is shown here, in the details, rather than in the main numbers above.
How prices are fetched
  • Web mode: the browser asks Yahoo's chart endpoint through a public CORS proxy, one symbol at a time, trying the next proxy if one fails. If every route fails for a symbol, its last saved price is kept and marked stale, and you can always type a price in by hand.
  • Intraday: the price used is Yahoo's regularMarketPrice — the live intraday quote while the market is open, the last close when it isn't. The previous close is only used for the day-change column, never as the price itself. Each holding shows whether the market was open and the quote's own timestamp.
  • Prices and their fetch time are saved in this browser, so reopening the page shows the last update and its timestamp rather than silently refetching.
